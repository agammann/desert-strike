DESERT STRIKE: RETURN TO THE GULF
Standalone fan recreation by agammann

Extract this entire ZIP, then open Desert-Strike.html in a modern browser.
On Windows you can also double-click Play.cmd.
No internet, ROM, emulator, installation, or server is required.

Default flight (From Above): WASD or arrows move in compass directions.
Classic With Momentum mode: W/S forward/reverse, A/D turn.
Weapons: J chain gun, K or Space Hydra, L Hellfire.
Hover over people and supplies to pick them up automatically.
Return crew to the frigate (H on the map) to unload and repair armor.
M opens the map and pauses. P or Escape pauses/resumes.
From Above controls are available in the briefing for easier flying.

Standard mode requires supply pickups for fuel and ammunition.
Relaxed mode also resupplies at the frigate.
No mid-campaign save: use pause or restart the campaign from its briefing.

This is a newly implemented recreation, not an exact port of the original.
Full instructions and buildable source:
