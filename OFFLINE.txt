DESERT STRIKE: RETURN TO THE GULF
Standalone fan recreation by agammann

Extract this entire ZIP, then open Desert-Strike.html in a modern browser.
On Windows you can also double-click Play.cmd.
No internet, ROM, emulator, installation, or server is required.

Default: Standard difficulty / With Momentum controls.
W/S forward/reverse, A/D turn; Shift + A/D strafe without turning.
Optional From Above: WASD or arrows move in compass directions.
Weapons: J chain gun, K or Space Hydra, L Hellfire.
Hover over people and supplies to pick them up automatically.
Deliver passengers at a landing zone (L) or the frigate (H) to repair armor.
M opens the map and pauses. P or Escape pauses/resumes.
From Above controls are available in the briefing for easier flying.

Standard mode requires supply pickups for fuel and ammunition.
Buildings cause collision damage. Disable radar/power to weaken local defenses.
Quick winch, extra lives, and some fuel/ammo are hidden under cache buildings.
Use the map Display selector to find visible supplies and mission targets.
Select a copilot in the briefing. Rescue Valdez and deliver him to the frigate
to unlock Jake for later operations.
Relaxed mode also resupplies at the frigate.
Follow the numbered mission briefing through 27 objectives in four campaigns.
Capture intelligence to reveal targets. Watch missile and rescue countdowns.
Protect embassy officials while boarding, clear the gate, and escort the bus.
Watch for helicopter reinforcements and the armor ambush.
At the palace, follow the occupied ATV and hold fire until the copilot exits.
Destroy the empty vehicle, breach the bomber, rescue him, then finish the plane.
Enable Sound for original briefing/result music and in-flight effects.
See THIRD_PARTY.md for artwork and music credits and rights information.
After all required missions are complete, hover at the frigate to finish.
A completed operation saves the next campaign and rounded score in this
browser when storage is available. Continue operation restores this checkpoint.
No mid-campaign save: use pause or restart the campaign from its briefing.

This is a newly implemented recreation, not an exact port of the original.
